# Research: small daily-puzzle-game monetization & distribution (Sept 4, 2026)

Produced by a Sonnet research sub-agent (WebSearch). Treat numbers as ballpark; several sites could not be fetched directly from this sandbox, so verify links before relying on them.

## 1. Monetization paths that work at small scale

- **Google AdSense**: effectively unusable pre-traffic; slow, uncertain approval for new sites.
- **Ezoic**: no hard traffic minimum ("Access Now" self-serve under 10K pageviews/mo). Real RPM lift needs volume. https://facileway.com/ezoic-requirements/ · https://cropink.com/best-ad-networks-for-small-publishers
- **Monetag**: better at ~10K–50K visits/mo; $5 payout threshold. https://monetag.com/blog/ezoic-alternatives/
- **Adsterra**: no minimum traffic, easiest approval for brand-new sites; low RPM until volume. https://adsterra.com/blog/ezoic-alternatives/
- **RPM reality**: gaming display RPM ~$2–6 per 1,000 sessions; one HTML5 game site reported ~$1.70 blended. https://toolsignal.site/articles/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026 · https://www.adpushup.com/blog/HTML5-game-ads/
  - Napkin math: $150/mo from ads alone needs ~50K–75K monthly sessions. Ads are a supplement, not the lever.
- **One-time Stripe "Pro" unlock**: sub-$50 one-time purchases convert in low single digits; prompt at high-intent moments (streak, wall, exhausted free content). https://stripe.com/resources/more/gaming-monetization-explained · https://dev.to/whoffagents/stripe-for-indie-developers-everything-i-learned-across-6-products-1050
  - Napkin math: $200/mo at $3–5 and 1–2% conversion ≈ 2,000–7,000 unique monthly visitors with real daily retention.
- **Ko-fi / Patreon**: goodwill supplement after a loyal base exists; not a launch driver.
- **Bottom line**: free core game + cheap one-time unlock as the primary lever; ads as a trickle once traffic exists; tips as upside.

## 2. Solo-dev daily puzzle examples and distribution

- **Clues by Sam** (May 2025): Show HN → Aftermath / Boing Boing / Kotaku → aggregators (Thinky Games, Playlin) → 50K+ DAU by early 2026, monetized via puzzle-pack purchases. https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43923843 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam · https://aftermath.site/clues-by-sam-wordle-daily-puzzle-game/
- **Lineup Puzzle**, **Daily Unfold**: Show HN launches 2025/2026, no revenue data. https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45301750 · https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47492770
- **Raddle** (word-transformation ladder with cryptic clues): local press, "Best Word Game 2025" nomination. https://blockclubchicago.org/2025/08/25/is-raddle-the-next-wordle-chicago-puzzle-makers-word-ladder-game-could-be-your-next-addiction/
- Pattern: Show HN → puzzle press → aggregator listings → share-grid word of mouth. No confirmed CrazyGames/Poki/TikTok success for this genre.
- Unverified: "$50K/month Wordle clone" podcast claim. Do not anchor on it.

## 4. Is "word ladder" crowded?

Yes, moderately. Competitors: Weaver (weavergame.org/.net + Play Store), wordladder.com, ladder.game, Wordstopia, DKM Word Ladder, wordruns.com, Sporcle/JetPunk ladder quizzes. Raddle shows the format still breaks out with a fresh hook.

Gap (hypothesis): existing ladder sites are dated/ad-heavy with weak streak/share mechanics. A mobile-first, Wordle-grade share loop plus a distinctive twist is the differentiator. Plain Weaver clone = saturated.

## 5. Legal / compliance minimums

- Privacy policy (disclose localStorage, Stripe, ad networks, analytics). https://www.termsfeed.com/blog/mobile-game-consent-requirements/
- Cookie consent banner with accept/reject per purpose if ads/analytics use tracking cookies and EU visitors exist. https://www.didomi.io/blog/video-game-consent-banners · https://usercentrics.com/resources/gdpr-checklist-for-mobile-games/
- COPPA: general-audience is fine, but avoid behavioral ad targeting of unverified users. https://www.cookiehub.com/coppa
- Terms of service with refund language once Stripe is live.
